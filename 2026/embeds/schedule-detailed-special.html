2026-09-01 10:30:00 11:30:00 Room 4 Special track Focus session: From data to discovery: expanding the frontiers of computational biology Theo Sanderson, Angie Hinrichs, Nick Loman Advances pioneered by computational biologists are redefining what is possible in data analysis, visualization, and collaboration. This session will highlight transformative approaches that have enabled analyses at unprecedented scale, from phylogenetics, to population-level genomics, and federated data infrastructures. Beyond their technical accomplishment, these tools can catalyze conceptual and cultural shifts: how can they reshape the questions we ask, the expectations of what we can achieve, and the next challenges we face.
2026-09-01 15:15:00 16:15:00 Room 4 Special track Focus session: International women in computational biology: stories & journeys Shilpa Garg, Ana Conesa Computational biology has been shaped by the contributions of women across academia, healthcare, industry, and entrepreneurship. This focus session will bring together leading scientists and innovators to share their personal and professional journeys, discuss career development, leadership, mentorship, and explore opportunities and challenges within the field.

The session aims to foster mentorship, strengthen professional networks, increase the visibility of women role models, and promote a more inclusive computational biology community. Through scientific perspectives and personal experiences, speakers will highlight diverse pathways to success and leadership in computational biology.

2026-09-02 11:30:00 13:00:00 Room 4 Special track Career workshop Valérie Barbié, Fabian Birzele, Moustafa Houmani, Livia Parodi, Erik Studer , Sandra Reinders, Dala Egger, Vanessa Monteiro, Andrea Cavallini, Thomas Collin, Emma Duncan Organized by the SIB People & Culture, this career workshop offers an open forum for early-career researchers to engage with professionals working across academia, industry, and the public sector. 2026-09-02 13:15:00 14:15:00 Room 4 Special track Focus session: Digital earth, living planet: biodiversity and climate in the age of big data This session will explore how large-scale data infrastructures are transforming our understanding of biodiversity and climate systems. It will highlight advances in aggregating, harmonising, and analysing massive environmental datasets to support evidence-based research and policy. Complementary perspectives will connect biodiversity informatics with emerging digital twin and Earth system modelling approaches. Together, the talks will emphasise the power of integrated big data frameworks to address interconnected ecological and climate challenges.
