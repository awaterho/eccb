    Poster presentations

    Thank you for presenting a poster at ECCB 2026. Poster sessions are an important part of the scientific programme and provide an excellent opportunity to share your work and engage with the computational biology community.

    Poster requirements

    Presenters must:

    • bring a printed A0 portrait poster (84,1 × 118,9 cm / 33.1 × 46.8 in);
    • present it in person during their assigned session; and
    • remain at your poster throughout the session. If your session takes place during lunchtime, please make sure to take the opportunity to eat as well.

    Posters must be in English and must not exceed the stated dimensions. Presenters are responsible for bringing their poster already printed; on-site printing will not be provided.

    For maximum readability, we recommend:

    • keeping text to a minimum and emphasising figures and diagrams;
    • using sufficiently large fonts;
    • choosing high-contrast, colour-blind-friendly colours; and
    • including a QR code linking to your publication, preprint, code repository or project website, where appropriate.

    Poster sessions

    ECCB 2026 will feature three dedicated poster sessions, each lasting 90 minutes. Every poster will be assigned to one of these sessions.

    Poster session Date Presentation time Set-up Removal
    Poster session 1 Monday 31 August 12:00–13:30 9:00-12:00 13:30-17:00
    Poster session 2 Tuesday 1 September 16:15–17:45 8:45-16:15 17:45
    Poster session 3 Wednesday 2 September 11:30–13:00 9:00-11:30 13:00-14:15

    Poster-session assignments, final poster numbers and floor plans can be found in the poster programme. Your EasyChair submission number is not your poster number, and poster numbers may be subject to change. If you are unsure of your assigned session, please refer to your abstract notification email or to the Poster Information desk during the poster sessions.

    For an overview of all sessions and activities taking place throughout ECCB 2026, consult the full interactive schedule.

    Finding your poster board

    The poster sessions will take place across two floors in Espace Ruth Dreifuss (ground floor) and Espace Lise Girardin (first floor).

    The floor plans below show the distribution of poster boards by scientific area and poster-number range.

    Each poster board will be identified by a scientific area abbreviation and poster number. Please:

    • check your final poster number before displaying your poster;
    • use the mounting materials provided on site;
    • install and remove your poster during the designated periods;
    • arrive at least five minutes before your session begins; and
    • remain at your poster throughout the session.

    Printed poster lists and floor plans will also be displayed at the venue. Posters remaining after the removal deadline will be collected and kept at the Poster information desk, available in Espace Lise Girardin (first floor) during each session to answer questions and assist presenters.

    Online poster platform

    The ECCB 2026 online poster platform will open shortly. Presenters will be able to upload:

    • a PDF of their poster; and
    • an optional MP4 video of up to five minutes.

    Uploading these materials is not required to participate as a poster presenter. However, presenters with submissions within the five conference scientific areas and the general computational biology track wishing to qualify for a poster prize must upload a PDF of their poster by 20 August 2026.

    Uploaded materials will be publicly accessible through the ECCB 2026 website.

    Uploading your poster files

    Once the upload platform opens, you will receive an email from no-reply@iscb.org with the subject line “You have been added as an Admin.”

    To upload your files, please log in to the virtual conference platform using the same email address you used to confirm your participation as an ECCB 2026 poster presenter and the password provided in the email. Then click My Profile in the top navigation menu. You may upload either or both of the following:

    To upload a video:

    1. Select Upload Video.
    2. Click the upload-video icon.
    3. Enter a recording name and select your video file.
    4. Click Submit.

    To upload a PDF of your poster:

    1. Select Edit Poster Page under My Profile.
    2. Scroll down to Poster PDF/Image and click Add File.
    3. Upload your PDF and select it from the file list.
    4. Click Select in the bottom-right corner.

    Poster prizes

    ECCB 2026 will award:

    • one poster prize in each of the conference’s five scientific areas; and
    • one poster prize in the general computational biology category.

    To be eligible, presenters must:

    • upload a PDF of their poster to the online poster gallery by 20 August 2026 (currently being set up; instructions will follow); and
    • present their poster in person during their assigned session.

    Conference participants will vote during the poster sessions by scanning the QR codes displayed on the poster boards. Winners will be announced during the Closing ceremony and must be present to receive their award.

    About SIB

    The SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics is an internationally recognized non-profit organization, dedicated to biological and biomedical data science. Its data scientists are passionate about creating knowledge and solving complex questions in many fields, from biodiversity and evolution to medicine. They provide essential databases and software platforms as well as bioinformatics expertise and services to academic, clinical, and industry groups. SIB federates the Swiss bioinformatics community of some 800 scientists, encouraging collaboration and knowledge sharing.
