    The last newsletter is out !

    Posted on 13 August 2026

    The European Conference for Computational Biology (ECCB) is returning to Switzerland. From 31 August to 4 September 2026, ECCB 2026 will take place in Geneva, a global hub at the intersection of science, diplomacy, and innovation.

    Organized by the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, ECCB 2026 will bring together the international computational biology community for five days of cutting-edge science, exchange, and collaboration. Researchers, clinicians, data scientists, and industry experts from around the world will meet to explore the latest advances in bioinformatics and computational biology, and to shape the future of data-driven life sciences.

    About SIB

    The SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics is an internationally recognized non-profit organization, dedicated to biological and biomedical data science. Its data scientists are passionate about creating knowledge and solving complex questions in many fields, from biodiversity and evolution to medicine. They provide essential databases and software platforms as well as bioinformatics expertise and services to academic, clinical, and industry groups. SIB federates the Swiss bioinformatics community of some 800 scientists, encouraging collaboration and knowledge sharing.
