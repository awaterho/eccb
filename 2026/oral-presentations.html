    Oral presentations

    Thank you for presenting at ECCB 2026. To help ensure a smooth running of the scientific programme, please read the instructions below carefully.

    Find your session  

    Please check your presentation time, room allocation and session details.

    🟦 View parallel sessions schedule

    🟦 View schedule at a glance


    Presentation guidelines

    Presentation language: English

    Presentation duration:

    • Proceedings talks: 15-minute presentation followed by 3 minutes for questions
    • Highlight talks: 15-minute presentation followed by 3 minutes for questions
    • Invited and Keynote talks: please refer to the Schedule at a glance for the date, time, room and duration of your presentation.

    Presentation slides

    • Aspect ratio: 16:9.
    • Accepted formats: PowerPoint (.pptx) and PDF.
    • Slide design: Please ensure your slides are colour-blind friendly. For guidance, see the Poster presentations.
    • Submission deadline: All presentations must be submitted to the Speakers' Preview Centre (Speakers service centre) as early as possible and no later than 2 hours before your scheduled presentation.
    • File naming: Please name your presentation file using the following format: Room_Day_Time_LastName (e.g. AD_Tuesday_1015_Smith.pptx or AD_Tuesday_1015_Smith.pdf).
    • Videos: If your presentation includes videos, please submit each video as a separate MP4 file, named Room_Day_Time_LastName_video1.mp4 (e.g. AD_Tuesday_1015_Smith_video1.mp4).

     Speaker preview centre opening hours: 

    • Sunday 30 August: 16:00–18:00
    • Monday 31 August: 08:00–17:30
    • Tuesday 1 September: 08:00–17:30
    • Wednesday 2 September: 08:00–16:00

    On the day of your presentation

    • Please arrive at your session room at least 10 minutes before the start of your session.
    • Upon arrival, please introduce yourself to the Session Chairs.
    • The front row, next to the Session Chair's desk, will be reserved for presenters.
    • Presentations must be delivered in person using the conference computer. The use of personal laptops during scientific sessions is not permitted.
    • Please respect your allocated presentation time.
    • A presentation clicker will be provided to advance your slides.
    • A confidence monitor displaying your current slide will be available on stage.

    Important information

    • Check your presentation time and room in the Parallel Sessions Schedule.
    • Upload your presentation at the Speaker Preview Center at least 2 hours before your presentation.
    • Be in your session room 10 minutes before the start of your session.
    • Please respect your allocated presentation time (15-minute presentation + 3-minute discussion) for proceedings and highlight talks. 

    About SIB

    The SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics is an internationally recognized non-profit organization, dedicated to biological and biomedical data science. Its data scientists are passionate about creating knowledge and solving complex questions in many fields, from biodiversity and evolution to medicine. They provide essential databases and software platforms as well as bioinformatics expertise and services to academic, clinical, and industry groups. SIB federates the Swiss bioinformatics community of some 800 scientists, encouraging collaboration and knowledge sharing.
