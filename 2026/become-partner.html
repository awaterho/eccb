    Partner with #GenevaLovesData: invest in the future of trusted data

    ECCB 2026, part of the #GenevaLovesData initiative, offers a unique platform to position your organization at the forefront of data-driven science, innovation and policy

    Partnership brochure


    ECCB 2026, Europe’s leading conference in computational biology, will take place in Geneva from 31 August to 4 September 2026, bringing together over 1,000 leading scientists, innovators, industry representatives, and policymakers in data-driven life sciences.

    Supported by the Swiss Confederation and organized by the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, Switzerland’s leading organization for biological and biomedical data science, ECCB is a flagship event of the #GenevaLovesData initiative.

    Whether your objective is scientific positioning, talent engagement, innovation visibility, or societal impact, we offer flexible partnership opportunities tailored to your goals:

    • ECCB visibility: direct engagement with a high-level international scientific audience in computational biology, AI, and life sciences (see offer)
    • #GenevaLovesData visibility: extended outreach beyond the conference, connecting partners with policymakers, institutions, media, and the wider public ecosystem in Geneva (see offer)

    About SIB

    The SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics is an internationally recognized non-profit organization, dedicated to biological and biomedical data science. Its data scientists are passionate about creating knowledge and solving complex questions in many fields, from biodiversity and evolution to medicine. They provide essential databases and software platforms as well as bioinformatics expertise and services to academic, clinical, and industry groups. SIB federates the Swiss bioinformatics community of some 800 scientists, encouraging collaboration and knowledge sharing.
